The Mehndi ceremony is often the most vibrant and energetic event of a Pakistani wedding. In recent years, couples have increasingly turned to digital invitations to set the perfect tone for their dholki and mehndi nights.

Here are the top 5 trends we are seeing on **ShaadiLink** right now:

## 1. Interactive Scratch-to-Reveal Cards

Why send a static image when you can make your guests participate? The scratch-to-reveal feature has become incredibly popular. Guests receive a digital card and must virtually "scratch" off a colorful overlay to reveal the Mehndi date and venue. It builds excitement and gets everyone talking before the event even begins.

## 2. Vibrant Marigold and Green Themes

While pastel weddings are having a moment for Walimas, the Mehndi is all about staying true to tradition. Bright yellows, rich marigold oranges, and deep emerald greens remain the absolute favorite color palettes. These colors translate beautifully onto mobile screens, making your digital invitation pop.

## 3. Background Music Integration

Music is the soul of a Mehndi. Adding an autoplaying, joyful dhol or sitar track to your digital invitation instantly puts guests in a festive mood as soon as they open the link. 

## 4. Personalized Guest Links

Instead of mass-forwarding a single PDF on WhatsApp, modern couples are generating unique links for each guest (or family). When Uncle Tariq opens his link, it says *"Tariq Family, please join us..."*. This level of personalization makes guests feel truly special.

## 5. Built-in RSVP Tracking

Say goodbye to manual spreadsheets. The best digital invitations now include built-in RSVP buttons, allowing guests to confirm their attendance directly from the invitation. This is a lifesaver for planning catering and seating arrangements.
